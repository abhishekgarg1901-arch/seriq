Our story

We didn't set out to make beauty.
We set out to make proof.

Sériq was born in New Delhi from a belief that luxury and honesty don't have to be a trade-off — and that the most beautiful thing a brand can offer is the truth.

The beginning

It started, as most good things do, with a quiet frustration — and a bathroom shelf full of broken promises.

For years, shopping for skincare in India meant choosing between two compromises. On one side, mass-market brands shouting about miracle actives — at concentrations so low they could never deliver, wrapped in claims no one could verify. On the other, imported luxury houses charging extraordinary sums for elegant bottles and beautiful stories, while telling you remarkably little about what was actually inside.

Our founders — a cosmetic formulator and a designer, both tired of squinting at ingredient lists — kept asking the same question. Why should buying something for your own skin require this much faith? Why couldn't a brand simply tell you the active, the percentage, and the evidence, and let the results speak?

So they built the brand they couldn't find. One that treats its customer as intelligent. One that earns trust by giving it away.

The name

A serum,
sharpened to a point.

The word Sériq began as serum — the most honest object in skincare, a concentrated dose of active with nowhere to hide. We sharpened it, ending on a hard, clean consonant, until it sounded like what it stands for: precise, potent, considered.

The accent on the é is deliberate — the single warm, human mark on an otherwise clinical word. It is, in a sense, the whole brand in one character: rigour, with a soul.

Say it once and you remember it. That was the point.

"Most luxury beauty asks you to believe. We'd rather ask you to read."
— The founding principle of Sériq

At Sériq, the experience of receiving your order matters as much as the products inside. We pack every parcel with care and partner with trusted couriers to bring it to you safely. This policy explains how and when your order will reach you.

Where we ship

We currently ship across India to all serviceable PIN codes. If our courier partners do not service your area, we will contact you with alternatives or issue a full refund.

  • Domestic: Available pan-India, including metro and non-metro locations.
  • International: Coming soon — see the international section below.

Order processing

Orders are processed Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

  • Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST are typically processed the same business day.
  • Orders placed after the cut-off, or on Sundays and holidays, are processed the next business day.
  • You will receive an order confirmation by email immediately, and a dispatch confirmation with tracking once your parcel leaves our facility.

We want you to feel confident in every Sériq purchase. If something isn't right, we'll do our best to make it so — within the limits that selling skincare responsibly requires.

Our promise

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, we will replace it or refund you in full — that is unconditional. Beyond that, the eligibility of a return depends on the condition of the product, for the hygiene reasons explained below.

What can be returned

  • Unopened & sealed products may be returned within 7 days of delivery, provided the outer seal is intact and the product is in its original, unused condition and packaging.
  • Damaged, defective or incorrect items are always eligible — see the dedicated section below.
  • Opened or used products are not eligible for return except where they are faulty, for the hygiene reasons below.
  • Gift sets must be returned complete; partial returns of a set are not accepted unless an item is faulty.
Why we can't accept opened skincare

Skincare and haircare are products applied directly to the body. Once a seal is broken, a product cannot be guaranteed safe or hygienic for anyone else, and so — like most reputable beauty brands — we are unable to accept returns of opened or used items unless they are faulty. This protects every customer, including you. We'd always rather you reach out before opening if you're unsure: our team is happy to help you choose.

How to return

Get in touch within 7 days

Email care@seriq.beauty with your order number and the reason for return.

Receive your return authorisation

We'll confirm eligibility and share return instructions and the address.

Pack it securely

Return the item in its original packaging, sealed and unused, with all components.

Ship it back

Send it via a trackable service. For eligible non-faulty returns, return shipping is the customer's responsibility; for faulty or incorrect items, we cover it.

Refunds

  • Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll email you to confirm approval.
  • Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval. Your bank or card provider may take additional time to reflect it.
  • Original shipping charges are non-refundable except where the return is due to our error or a faulty product.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are processed via bank transfer or store credit, as agreed with you.

Prefer not to wait? Where a return is eligible, we're happy to offer store credit or an exchange instead of a refund — often the faster option.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

We inspect every order before it leaves us, but if something slips through:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photographs of the item and packaging.
  • We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund, including any shipping you paid.
  • Please do not discard the product or packaging until your claim is resolved.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before they are dispatched. Once an order has been dispatched, it can no longer be cancelled, but eligible items may be returned as above.

Contact us

Our care team is here to help at care@seriq.beauty. We aim to respond within one business day, and we'd genuinely rather solve a problem than lose your trust.
